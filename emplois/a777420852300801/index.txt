Hobart is the leading supplier of equipment, systems and service in the food industry. Hobart offers the broadest line of equipment including cooking, food preparation, refrigeration, bakery systems, warewashing and waste systems, and weighing, wrapping and labeling systems. Headquartered in Toronto, Ontario with branch offices in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Montreal, Quebec, Toronto, Ontario, Calgary and Edmonton, Alberta, and Vancouver, British Columbia. The company employs 240 people. Included in this number are over 125 service technicians providing nationwide service, effectively making Hobart Canada has the largest service organization among manufacturers in the food equipment industry.

Job Description

The Regional Service Manager (RSM) is responsible for the overall operational, financial, and people leadership of specific service region. This role leads Service Area Managers, Service Advisors, and field service teams to ensure the consistent delivery of exceptional customer service, safe work practices, operational excellence, and profitable growth.

The Regional Service Manager acts as the primary leader of the service business within their assigned region and is accountable for achieving revenue, profitability, customer satisfaction, technician utilization, safety, and employee engagement objectives.

Success in this role requires a strong balance of leadership, business acumen, financial management, strategic planning, and customer relationship management. The Regional Service Manager must be equally comfortable reviewing financial forecasts, coaching managers, resolving customer escalations, developing talent, and supporting field operations. This position will be based in Richmond Hill, ON location, and will report directly to the Business Unit Manager.

Qualifications:

Diploma or Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Management, Engineering, Technical Services, or a related discipline preferred. Equivalent industry experience will be considered.

7+ years of service management, operations leadership, or technical field service experience.

Minimum 3-5 years leading managers, supervisors, or multi-site service operations.

Experience managing P&L responsibilities and operational budgets.

Experience leading large mobile workforces and field service teams.

Strong customer-facing leadership experience.

Responsibilities:

Strategic Planning

Develop regional strategies that align with corporate objectives.

Identify opportunities for operational improvement, market expansion, and service growth.

Support business transformation initiatives and organizational change.

Operational Leadership

Lead all day-to-day service operations across the assigned region.

Ensure service delivery standards are consistently met or exceeded.

Monitor workload, response times, call completion rates, technician productivity, and customer satisfaction metrics.

Work closely with dispatch and service management teams to ensure proper technician coverage and resource allocation.

Drive continuous improvement initiatives that improve efficiency, service quality, and profitability.

Ensure all service employees operate in accordance with company policies, procedures, and operational standards.

Customer Relationship Management
