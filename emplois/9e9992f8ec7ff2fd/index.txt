12:Tfa2,Materials Testing Technician The Materials Testing Technician performs the tests, inspections, sampling and follow-ups needed to ensure that materials and production activities meet requirements. This person works both in the laboratory and in the field, including testing fresh concrete, aggregates, and backfill and foundation materials. They maintain traceability of samples and test specimens, prepare the required reports, share results with the people involved, and make sure that deadlines, test methods and the laboratory's quality program are followed. What does a Materials Testing Technician do? Perform inspections, sampling and materials testing in the laboratory, at the plant and on construction sites; Perform tests on fresh concrete, including slump, air content, temperature and unit weight, and cast test specimens according to the applicable methods; Label, store, cure and manage hardened concrete specimens, then coordinate their preparation and shipment to the subcontracted laboratory while maintaining traceability; Perform tests on aggregates and soils, including:- Particle size analysis (sieve analysis);- Hydrometer analysis;- Moisture content and other required physical tests;- Standard Proctor and Modified Proctor tests;- Compaction and density measurements with a nuclear density gauge. Monitor production activities and check that requirements, work methods, deadlines and the quality program are followed; Record observations, test results, deviations and corrective actions in the designated forms and databases; Compile, analyze and validate results to quickly detect any trend or non-conformity;Write, complete and submit test, inspection and production reports within the required timeframes; Communicate by email or verbally with production teams, quality managers, subcontractors and other people involved; Work with production teams to quickly correct any quality deviations; Perform routine checks, preventive maintenance and cleaning of laboratory and field equipment; Keep the calibration and verification status of equipment up to date, in line with the laboratory's quality system and program; Report any defective or out-of-tolerance equipment, or equipment with expired calibration, right away and apply the required control measures;Keep work areas, equipment, samples and laboratory records clean, organized and compliant with health, safety and quality requirements; Support production operations and the laboratory manager as needed. What does it take to apply? College diploma (DEC) in civil engineering technology, certificate (AEC) in materials testing or related training, or equivalent experience; Experience in materials testing, in the laboratory or on site, is an asset (in-house training provided); Valid ASP Construction card, when required for site access; Knowledge of testing on fresh concrete, aggregates, soils and compacted materials is an asset (in-house training provided); Ability to operate a nuclear density gauge and meet the applicable safety, training and authorization requirements is an asset (in-house training provided); Strong computer skills, including Excel, Word, email and results entry systems; Ability to produce and interpret reports from the interface and database; Thoroughness, independence, strong observation skills, good judgment and attention to traceability; Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines; Ability to work in a team and communicate effectively with operations and external partners; Good physical condition and ability to work both in the laboratory and outdoors, depending on site conditions; Valid driver's licence and availability to travel to different sites as needed.
