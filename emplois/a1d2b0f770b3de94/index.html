Company Information

Hobart is the leading supplier of equipment, systems and service in the food industry. Hobart offers the broadest line of equipment including cooking, food preparation, refrigeration, bakery systems, warewashing and waste systems, and weighing, wrapping and labeling systems.

Headquartered in Toronto, Ontario with branch offices in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Montreal, Quebec, Toronto, Ontario, Edmonton, Alberta, and Vancouver, British Columbia. The company employs more than 240 people. Included in this number are over 125 service technicians providing nationwide service, effectively making Hobart Canada the industry’s largest service organization.

Hobart Food Equipment Group Canada is looking for an Appliance Repair Technician for GTA and surrounding area. Hobart is a well-established company providing competitive wages and benefits.

Responsibilities:

This position is for a commercial appliance repair technician, Previous experience in appliance repair industry residential / commercial a definite asset. This technician will perform field service consisting of adjusting, aligning, assembling, calibrating, inspecting, lubricating and testing of commercial food prep/kitchen equipment. Having a strong electrical knowledge and able to read wiring diagrams an asset as well as good troubleshooting skills and mechanical knowledge. The candidate would be required to work with limited supervision. An outgoing personality with strong communication skills and a desire to succeed in a customer focused environment would be an asset.

Other duties include:

Assisting others in equipment installations or in performing major repairs

Attending, completing service training schools and audiovisual field training, as required

Assisting in obtaining service/preventative maintenance contracts

Assisting in product demonstrations

Skills/abilities:

High school diploma (minimum)

College graduate of a Mechanical – Electrical – Electronic course would be an asset

Excellent mechanical/electrical/electronic aptitude

2-3 years of relative experience is an asset

Ability to learn new technology

Valid driver’s license with acceptable driving record

Able to travel to the USA and attend training schools

Excellent verbal, written and interpersonal communication skills

Be organized, accountable and self motivated

Excellent customer service skills

Preference will be given to those with a valid G2 gas certificate

We Offer:

Compensation $25.00 – $38.00+ per hour + applicable premiums base on relevant experience, technical competencies, certifications, etc.

Competitive Group Insurance Benefit Plans

Company Pension Plan/ with Company Match RSP

Company Provided: Service Vehicle, Laptop, Tablet, Cell phone

Please note: Criminal background and reference checks will be completed as a condition of employment.

We value diversity in our workforce and encourage all qualified candidates to apply. Disability related accommodation during the recruitment process is available upon request. We appreciate all responses and advise that only those candidates selected for an interview will be contacted. This posting for a current vacancy. AI will not be used to screen or assess applicants.
